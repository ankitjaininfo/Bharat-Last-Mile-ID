# Bharat Last-Mile ID: Stakeholder Benefits

Bharat Last-Mile ID is a next step in digital India. The proposed Level 1 focuses on 'digital adoption' amoung masses, and making everyday delivery interactions easier through signed autofill. 

## Delivery platforms

Stakeholders: courier services, groceries & food delivery apps, urban service, etc.
Examples: Swiggy, Zomato, DHL, BlueDart, Urban Company, YesMadam, etc

- Faster gate processing and information exchanges.
- The gate-pass is a verifiable entry evidence, or check-in.
- Fewer transcription/typing mistakes.
- Temper-proof timestamps for measuring gate-arrival/delivery commitments, and preventing thefts.
- Feedback loop to track the delivery person's delivery timmings. 
- One open presentation format instead of a private 1:1 integrations integrations.

## Apartment Management Apps / ERP

Stakeholders: ERP apps, gate checking services, etc.
Examples: ADDA Gate Keeper, MyGate, etc

- Faster, signed data intake while preserving existing entry and approval workflows.
- Automatic form filling and verification.
- Less bespoke work for every delivery-platform integration.
- Continued control over each property's curated building and unit directory.
- Easy decisions for allow or not allow an executive.

## Residential Welfare Associations (RWAs)

Stakeholders: Building and facility managers, associations

- Lesser queues at gate. 
- Verified entry of the delivery partners
- Residents don't need double approvals, when placing orders.
- Fewer disputes


## Delivery partners

- Less repeated dictation of name, phone number, company, vehicle, and destination at every gate.
- A verifiable platform-issued credential available after pickup.
- Faster gate interactions, more deliveries.

## Security guards

- Auto-filled information from a signed source rather than hurried verbal entry.
- Clearer destination candidates and fewer searches through inconsistent property directories.
- Optional spoken confirmation and localized interfaces.
- A visible distinction between what the issuer verified and what the property's own process approved.
- More time for observation, exception handling, and resident assistance instead of repetitive typing.
- Optional vehicle information that helps properties resolve parking issues.

## Consumers

Stakeholders: Residents, office recipients, etc

- Lesser notifications, clearer approval context.

## Digital India and the broader ecosystem

- An open credential format designed for independent implementation in a rapidly growing last-mile economy.
- Participation by smaller properties, local delivery firms, and new reader products.
- Reduced dependence on private one-to-one integrations.
- Reusable signed data exchange at physical touch points, with further privacy improvements scoped for Level 2.
- Compatibility with evolving Indian digital-address infrastructure without making any single map, vendor, or registry mandatory.
